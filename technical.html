Technical Library

The open, in-depth knowledge base behind the design. Canon-locked, economics-free, public.

What is the Technical Library?
A public technical knowledge base — roughly 1,005 pages across 22 categories — covering the physics, engineering and systems of the Kronos design in depth. It's where a concept on the main site opens into the full technical detail behind it. Browse it.
Is it free and public?
Yes — openly accessible, no login and no paywall. It's meant to be read, cited and checked.
Is it kept consistent with the frozen design?
Yes. Every entry is canon-locked to the ratified 2026-08-27 freeze, and it carries no economic or commercial content — physics and engineering only. When the design freeze updates, the library updates with it.
How is it organized?
Into 22 categories spanning plasma physics, magnets, neutronics, materials, tritium, control and balance-of-plant — so you can move from a term to the depth behind it without hunting.
How does it relate to the papers and the register?
The Technical Library is the readable reference layer; the 44-paper series is the formal record with DOIs, and the De-Risking Register is the gate-by-gate evidence. They cross-reference each other.
Can I cite it?
Yes — for formal citation, prefer the DOI-bearing papers and datasets (see Cite & Index); the library is the best place to understand a topic before citing its primary source.
