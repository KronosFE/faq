About Kronos Fusion Energy

Who we are and what we’re building.

What is Kronos Fusion Energy?
A fusion energy company — isotopes first, electricity next — building toward first construction in 2027.
Why “isotopes first”?
The breeder produces tritium, helium-3 and 14-MeV neutrons — bankable isotopes — before the burner delivers firm electricity. It de-risks the path to power.
Who founded it?
Priyanca Iyengar Ford, Founder & CEO (publishing as P. I. Ford).
Are the results open?
Yes — 44 papers and a permanent De-Risking Register are openly published; every headline number is computed from first principles.
How many machines?
Two: the breeder (Hyperion) and the burner (Aegis and MetroVolt housings).
