About Kronos

The questions people ask first — answered plainly, canon-locked to the ratified freeze, with no cost or funding figures.

What is Kronos Fusion Energy building?
Two machines. The Hyperion breeder is a spherical tokamak built as a materials platform — its job is tritium and isotopes. The Aegis / MetroVolt burner is a D-³He tandem mirror, one core in two housings (defense installations and datacenter power). Together they lead with what a fusion machine makes on the way to electricity.
Is it operating yet?
No. Kronos is at the conceptual design and simulation stage. The design has been re-derived from first principles — 164 binding physics gates re-run on established codes — and ratified in the 2026-08-27 freeze. It is not an operating plant, and we say so on every page.
Why lead with materials instead of electricity?
Net-electricity breakeven is the right long-term goal and the hardest one — a single, distant gate. Tritium and isotopes have real markets today, so leading with them turns one far-off milestone into a product that closes on tritium self-sufficiency. See the wedge.
What's the difference between the breeder and the burner?
The breeder (Hyperion) is a spherical tokamak whose gate is tritium self-sufficiency (breeding ratio up to 1.42 on-model) — it makes no net-electricity claim. The burner (Aegis/MetroVolt) is a low-neutron D-³He tandem mirror that closes on-model at engineering gain Q_E 1.318. Function first, always.
What is the timeline?
On the current plan: construction from 2027, first tritium around 2030-31, a WHAM-scale burner demonstration in 2032, and fleet build from 2036. Every date is a target tied to a gate, not a commitment — see the roadmap.
Is the work public and checkable?
Yes. The De-Risking Register (gate-checked), 141 interactive validators, the CGYRO dataset and a 44-paper series are all published with permanent DOIs. Independent reproduction is explicitly welcome — see reproducibility.
Is this "AI-modeled" fusion?
No. The physics comes from established, community-benchmarked codes. AI/ML sits alongside as method — surrogates, control, prediction — never as the source of the physics. A headline number is what a physics code computed, not what a model guessed. See how we compute.
