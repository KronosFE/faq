The Engineering Blueprint

What the complete engineering design is, and what’s public vs. Kronos-team-only.

What is the Kronos Engineering Blueprint?
A complete, computed engineering design — the full drawing set across three machines, every dimension derived from first principles and cross-checked against the analysis campaign.
Can I see it?
An overview is public (scale, structure, and openly-published performance). The drawings themselves — geometry, dimensions, materials and specifications — are Kronos-team-only.
Three products?
Yes: Hyperion (the breeder), and Aegis & MetroVolt (two housings of the burner). The blueprint is organized by machine and subsystem.
How big is it?
Over 400 CAD parts modeled, a 400-plus-line bill of materials, and roughly 200 suppliers screened.
Is it real, or a concept?
It is a real, computed engineering design — we are a fusion energy company building toward first construction in 2027.
