Investors

The thesis, the stage, and how to engage. Financial detail is shared privately under NDA — not on this page.

What is the Kronos investment thesis?
Lead with materials, not electricity. Tritium and isotopes have real markets before net-electricity breakeven, so value can arrive earlier and the path is de-risked — backed by an open, checkable evidence base (164 gates, published with DOIs) and an experienced team. See the wedge.
What stage is the program at?
Conceptual design and simulation — the design re-derived from first principles, 164 physics gates re-run, a ratified freeze in place — on a milestone-gated path toward construction. See the roadmap; dates are targets tied to gates.
What does the due-diligence package cover?
Three pillars: technical DD (the open de-risking register, V&V, and code deep-dives), independently verified team DD, and the IP portfolio. Financial and cap-table detail is provided separately under NDA.
Where are the financials and valuation?
In a private, NDA-gated data room — not on the public site. This page carries no cost, funding or valuation figures by design; serious investors receive that detail through the diligence process.
What is the IP position?
Kronos holds granted and pending intellectual property across the machine and its control architecture. Specifics are covered in the DD process rather than published here.
What makes the evidence base different?
Most of the story a diligence team wants — the physics, the codes, the reproducibility — is already public and checkable, not gated. That lets technical diligence start immediately; the private material is the commercial and financial layer. See Science & Evidence.
How do investors engage?
Through the contact page. Serious inquiries are routed into the private diligence process.
