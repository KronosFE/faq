Careers

Working at Kronos — who we look for and how to reach us.

Is Kronos hiring?
Kronos is a growing team across founding science partners, the board, scientific advisors, auditors and operations. We welcome interest through the careers application at any time — strong people reach out before a role is posted.
How do I apply?
Through the careers application form. You submit your details and a link to your résumé (a hosted link rather than an upload). The form can be pre-filled for a specific role if you're applying to one.
What backgrounds do you look for?
Plasma physics; fusion and nuclear engineering; materials; high-performance computing and numerical methods; controls and software; and operations & commercialization — alongside strong generalists who can work across disciplines.
What is the team like?
Fusion and magnet pioneers, a scientific advisory board that includes a Turing Award laureate, and a dedicated Scientific Auditors tier whose job is to check the science. Every bio is independently verified — see the team.
Do you take internships or academic collaborations?
Reach out through the application or contact and tell us what you'd bring — the program spans research, engineering and software.
Where can I learn about the work first?
Start with Science & Evidence and How We Compute — the same open record we build on is the best picture of the work you'd be joining.
