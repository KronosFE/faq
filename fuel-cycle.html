The Fuel Cycle

What Kronos machines run on, and where the fuel comes from.

What fuel do the machines use?
The breeder runs on deuterium–tritium (D–T); the burner runs on deuterium–helium-3 (D–³He).
Where does the tritium come from?
The breeder breeds it in a lithium blanket — n + ⁶Li → T + ⁴He — which is why tritium self-sufficiency is its defining gate.
And the helium-3?
The breeder produces helium-3; over the long term it is also a lunar resource.
Why does this matter?
Tritium is scarce worldwide; a machine that breeds its own fuel and useful isotopes is the platform the rest of the program is built on.
