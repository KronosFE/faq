The breeder — Hyperion

How the spherical-tokamak breeder works, and why it's a materials platform. Canon-locked, no financials.

What is a breeder, and what does Hyperion breed?
Hyperion is a spherical tokamak designed as a materials platform. It breeds tritium — the fuel fusion needs and the world is short of — and produces isotopes. Its defining gate is tritium self-sufficiency, not net electricity.
Why a spherical tokamak, not a conventional one?
A low aspect ratio gives a compact, high-β configuration — more plasma pressure for the magnetic field. Paired with negative triangularity, it's a shape that holds heat well in a small machine. See the equilibrium.
What is negative triangularity and why does it matter?
It's a plasma cross-section whose corner points outward (δ = −0.30), the opposite of a conventional tokamak. Nonlinear gyrokinetics (CGYRO) shows this shape suppresses the turbulence that otherwise drives heat out — the single most consequential physics choice in the design.
How can it run without a central solenoid?
Most tokamaks use a central solenoid to drive current. Hyperion doesn't need one: a large bootstrap current from the plasma's own pressure gradient, topped up by RF current drive, sustains 9.66 MA non-inductively. Freeing the central column is what makes the compact spherical geometry possible. See current drive.
What is the tritium breeding ratio (TBR)?
The ratio of tritium the machine produces to the tritium it consumes. Above one means self-sufficient. Hyperion reaches up to 1.42 on-model, computed with OpenMC neutronics, with margin for the fuel cycle's losses (tracked with TMAP8).
Does Hyperion make net electricity?
No — and it makes no such claim. It is a platform whose success is measured in tritium and isotopes. That honest gate is stated on every page; net electricity is the burner's and the fleet's later game, not the breeder's promise.
What are the headline numbers?
Q 3.076 at 85.04 MW fusion power, 9.66 MA plasma current (solenoid-free), δ −0.30, α-confinement 99.98%, TBR up to 1.42 — canon-locked to the 2026-08-27 freeze. Explore them in the operating-point explorer.
