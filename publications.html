Publications

What Kronos publishes, where, and how to cite it. Open access, permanent DOIs, canon-locked.

What does Kronos publish?
A 44-paper design series, the Physics De-Risking Register of 164 gates, and supporting datasets — all with permanent DOIs and archived data. It's the formal record behind the design.
Is it open access?
Yes. The deposits are openly available on Zenodo with permanent DOIs and clear licensing (CC-BY), so they're citable and resolvable independent of this website — they don't depend on our servers staying up.
Is it peer-reviewed?
The work is published open-access with DOIs today; peer-reviewed journal submission is part of the publication plan. We state that honestly rather than claim review that hasn't happened — and everything is public so it can be scrutinized now.
Can the results be reproduced?
Yes. Headline numbers regenerate from named scripts and archived data under a fixed seed, and the open KRONOS Research Toolkit recomputes the design anchors on a clean install. See reproducibility.
What are the main DOIs?
De-Risking Register 10.5281/zenodo.22133057 · CGYRO dataset 22136279 · Paper 1.5 (AI/ML & Quantum) 22132484 · the book 22179328. Full list and BibTeX on Cite & Index.
How do I cite the work?
By DOI. The simplest starting citation: Ford, P. I. & Kronos Fusion Energy (2026). Kronos Physics De-Risking Register v1.0. doi:10.5281/zenodo.22133057. BibTeX for every deposit is on Cite & Index.
Is there a book?
Yes — The Fusion Energy Equations, the full design series as one volume, with a permanent DOI and a web edition. See the book.
