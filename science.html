Science & de-risking

How the design is known to work, what tools produced it, and how you can check it. Canon-locked, no financials.

How do you know the design works?
Every binding physics question was re-run from first principles on established codes and tied to a numbered gate in an open register — 164 gates, with 141 interactive validators that recompute on demand. The full method is in How We Compute, and how each result is qualified is in the V&V matrix.
Which codes do you use?
The same open, community-benchmarked tools the field uses: CGYRO (turbulence), OpenMC (neutronics), ASCOT5 (fast ions), FreeGS (equilibrium), NIMROD (MHD), CQL3D·GENRAY (current drive), TMAP8 (tritium), ALARA·TALYS·NJOY (activation), Xolotl·RustBCA (materials) and Quantum-ESPRESSO (DFT). No proprietary physics engine.
Is it peer-reviewed and reproducible?
The design is published as a 44-paper series with permanent DOIs and archived data, so headline numbers regenerate from named scripts under a fixed seed. The open KRONOS Research Toolkit recomputes the design anchors on a clean install. Independent reproduction is welcomed — see reproducibility.
Is this "AI-modeled" fusion?
No. The physics comes from the established codes. The AI/ML layer sits alongside as method — surrogates that speed the search, a control stack, disruption prediction — never as the source of the physics. A quoted number is a full-physics calculation, not a model's guess. See the methods layer.
What is the De-Risking Register?
A public register of the 164 binding physics gates — the claim, the code and the basis for each — with a permanent DOI (10.5281/zenodo.22133057). It's the spine the whole program hangs on. Open it.
How were the confinement claims cross-checked?
The negative-triangularity confinement benefit was re-run with nonlinear gyrokinetics not only on our own geometry but on representative DIII-D-NT and TCV-like shapes (from published values) — a first-principles cross-check on independent shapes. We're explicit that these are representative geometries, not named-shot reconstructions.
Where should I start if I'm a physicist?
How We Compute for the method, V&V & Uncertainty for the qualification, Theory & Methods for the equations, and The Codes for the per-code deep-dives.
