AI-Native Architecture

How control, the digital twin, and optimization are designed into Kronos from the start.

What does “AI-native” mean here?
Control, the digital twin, and design optimization are built in from the start — not bolted on. The plasma-control architecture, the live digital twin of the machine, and the design-optimization loop are ML-driven.
Do you use AI to control the plasma?
The control architecture is designed for real-time, ML-assisted plasma control and disruption avoidance. It is validated against first-principles simulation before it ever touches hardware.
Where does quantum computing fit?
We use quantum methods (e.g. Qiskit) for specific materials and physics problems. The heavy physics runs today on an NVIDIA GPU HPC platform (A100 / H100); quantum is the frontier we study for what a real QPU could extend.
Is the AI trained on real data?
The models are built and validated against first-principles simulations and the published de-risking results — every figure is computed, not assumed.
How does this connect to the digital twin?
A live digital twin mirrors the frozen engineering point; see the digital-twin FAQ for how it is built and secured.
